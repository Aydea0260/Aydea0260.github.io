Internal project

Cloning Minds

Cloning Minds is an internal project of Sandbox Games that creates intelligent virtual avatars able to recreate knowledge, processes, methodologies and specialised forms of human interaction.

It takes technologies from videogame development, Character AI, simulation and virtual worlds and applies them to specific human functions. The goal is not a visual copy of a person but an intelligent digital presence — an Intelligent Virtual Human.

What a Cloning Mind can combine

  • Visual identity3D, photorealistic or stylised avatar; virtual character; VR / AR / metaverse representation.
  • VoiceSynthetic or personalised voice; intonation, language, rhythm, conversational style.
  • KnowledgeDocuments, books, courses, research, protocols, manuals, methodologies and authorised expert content.
  • ProcessesHow a person explains, structures a class, asks questions, runs a session and guides an activity.
  • MemoryContext across prior conversations, progress, preferences, goals, sessions and accumulated learning.

Cloning Minds can create:

  • Virtual teachers and tutors
  • Virtual psychologists (with professional supervision)
  • Virtual coaches and mentors
  • Corporate trainers and virtual consultants
  • Digital experts and virtual presenters
  • Course facilitators and AI guides
  • Specialised NPCs, digital humans and knowledge-oriented digital twins

How one avatar evolves across media

Text AI→Voice AI→Video avatar→3D avatar→VR character→AR character→Persistent metaverse human

The intelligence can stay consistent while the interface changes.

Clinical governance — When Cloning Minds avatars are used in clinical or mental-health contexts they must include professional supervision, clear limits of use, privacy, security, escalation protocols, governance and a clear distinction between digital support and human clinical care.
