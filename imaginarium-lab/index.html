What Imaginarium Lab is

Imaginarium Lab is an independent creative brand spanning music, children’s and teen storytelling, and kids’ merchandise.

Its music is distributed by DistroKid under the label 13622897 Records DK.

It released the album All My Colors (13 tracks, 3 September 2026) and Chef of My Heart (6 tracks, 4 September 2026), plus the single Ocean Eyes of Green.

Sebastián Santana is credited on its compositions and production; the founder / director title is pending an independent public source.
