The claim

What Psykhê Paths is

Psykhê Paths is a platform of personalized transformational paths that turns psychological, emotional and contextual information into structured processes of self-knowledge, wellbeing and personal development — without replacing clinical care. It was founded and is directed by Sebastian Santana, a clinical psychologist and the creator of the methodology.

Psykhê Paths is a platform of personalized transformational paths that turns psychological, emotional and contextual information into structured processes of self-knowledge, wellbeing and personal development, without replacing clinical care.

It was founded and is directed by Sebastian Santana, a clinical psychologist.

It receives general learnings and patterns from Psykhê Therapies while clinical data and processes are kept separate.

It produces personalized Paths, workshops, exercises, educational content and structured digital processes.

It connects to CoachMe for work-related matters and non-clinical emotions, and to Great Studios for audiovisual production.

Its methodology integrates psychology with symbolic self-knowledge maps (personality, attachment, psychological flexibility, Enneagram, Human Design, Jungian psychology), used as interpretive tools and not as scientific diagnoses.

The book The Designed Self is its theoretical foundation.

Its official website, commercial model and final AI implementation are not yet documented.

This Psykhê Paths is the personal-development and wellbeing platform in the Santana ecosystem; it is not clinical psychotherapy and not the same as Psykhê Therapies.
