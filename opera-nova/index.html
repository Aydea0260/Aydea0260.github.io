Licensing

One Source. Every Version. No Drama.

Opera Nova is a named composer’s catalogue — not an anonymous library. Licensing opens in Phase 3 through sync libraries (Musicbed · Artlist · Pond5 · AudioJungle) and direct relationships with music supervisors.

01
Sync + master, together

One transaction covers both sides — no separate publisher chase.

02
The full version stack

Main, trailer, extended, short, ambient and radio/sync edits of every piece.

03
Full stems, always

Vocals · pads · percussion · bass · FX — reshape the cue to your cut.

04
Supervisor-ready specs

Duration, loudness and format printed on every release — WAV 24-bit / 48 kHz + MP3 320.

Licensing & commissions — via the channel
Stated plainly

Catalog licenses are non-exclusive; exclusive buyouts and custom commissions are quoted per project by the studio. Buying or streaming a track is not a license — when Phase 3 opens, every cue here will carry its license terms on its own page, including how Content ID claims are cleared.
