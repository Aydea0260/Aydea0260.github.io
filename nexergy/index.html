Entity: Organization — Nexergy (U.S. energy-infrastructure company).

Nexergy is a U.S.-based energy infrastructure company led by CEO Mateo Santana and CSO Sebastian Santana.

It specialises in power transformer inspection, diagnostics, testing, maintenance and repair, and in the supply and installation of solar photovoltaic systems.

Its clients are utility companies, transmission and distribution operators, industrial plants, large commercial facilities and critical infrastructure.

Its transformer field services include emergency response and on-site technical support.

Its solar service covers site assessment, system design, equipment supply, installation, commissioning and maintenance.

It operates mainly in Florida and Texas, with presence in Miami and Houston, and can execute additional U.S. projects subject to contracts, licences and operational capacity.

It runs a bilingual operation in English and Spanish and maintains an operational and commercial relationship with Latin America, particularly Colombia, for talent, suppliers, components and logistics.

Its work follows applicable OSHA, NFPA 70E, NEC (NFPA 70) and EPA requirements, plus IEEE, ANSI, NETA, utility and manufacturer standards depending on the project.
