InNOVAtion is a company that creates, implements and operates AI agents that work inside other companies.

It started as a software development company and evolved into building and deploying AI agents for businesses.

It offers three service models: custom development and implementation; Aydea CoWork; and pay-per-use.

Aydea CoWork is InNOVAtion’s “AI workforce as a service” line — agents hired for a monthly fee or by usage; it is a service line of InNOVAtion, not necessarily a separate company.

The agents integrate with CRM, ERP, email, messaging, documents and databases, and work alongside human teams who supervise their results.
