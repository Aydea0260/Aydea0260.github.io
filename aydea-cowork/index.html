The claim

What Aydea CoWork is

Aydea CoWork is a service that lets companies hire AI agents by monthly subscription or pay-per-use, without bearing the full cost of designing, developing, implementing and maintaining their own agents. It offers a flexible digital workforce. The agents are developed and operated by inNOVAtion [relationship structure to be documented].
