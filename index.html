Identity statement

Sebastian
Santana

Sebastian Santana is a Colombian psychologist, author and multidisciplinary creator whose work runs across mental health, therapeutic storytelling and music. He is the founder of Psykhe Therapies, has worked with Fundación Contacto Humano—the nonprofit behind the audio series Cuentos para Sanar—and is linked to the music project Imaginarium Lab (relationship pending public documentation).
