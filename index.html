Who Sebastián Santana is

Juan Sebastián Santana Herrera is a Colombian psychologist, author and multidisciplinary creator whose work runs across mental health, therapeutic storytelling and music. He is the founder of Psykhe Therapies, has worked with Fundación Contacto Humano — the nonprofit behind the audio series Cuentos para Sanar — and is the creator of the music project Imaginarium Lab [relationship pending public documentation], which has released the albums All My Colors and Chef of My Heart.
