The system

Editorial pipeline

Scriba's work runs through seven stages. Each stage is executed by several of the 40 specialised AI subagents.

  1. Discover & researchSpot topics, audiences and cultural needs; gather sources and context; verify claims and rate source quality; set purpose, reader, genre, format and sales channels.4 subagents
  2. Develop & writeTurn an idea into a differentiated proposal, design chapters and narrative arcs, write the manuscript, and organise the author's ideas while preserving their voice.4 subagents
  3. Edit & validateDevelopmental and line editing, proofreading, sensitivity reading, clinical / academic / technical review, and originality & rights checks.6 subagents
  4. Design & produceArt direction and visual continuity, illustration, editorial design, layout for print and ebook, cover, accessibility, pre-press QA, and bibliographic metadata.9 subagents
  5. Publish & distributePrepare KDP, print-on-demand and digital platforms; translate and localise; choose territories, formats and partners; set prices per format and territory.5 subagents
  6. Market & adaptLaunch calendars, editorial marketing and funnels, narrative storytelling, audiovisual adaptation with Great Studios, PR and alliances, reader community, sales, and derivative rights.8 subagents
  7. Measure, optimize & relaunchTrack sales, conversion, reviews and ad spend by territory; adjust metadata, pricing, campaigns and formats; keep the catalogue alive with re-editions; and archive versions, approvals and assets.4 subagents

40 specialised AI subagents run across these seven stages. Every title also has a coordinating editorial agent that assigns tasks, checks requirements and surfaces critical points for human approval.

Governance

Mandatory human controls

Even with an AI-intensive structure, these decisions always require human approval:

  • Final acceptance of the manuscript
  • Use of names, likeness and intellectual property
  • Sensitive medical, psychological, scientific, legal or educational content
  • Contracts, ISBN, rights and licences
  • Images of minors or identifiable people
  • Approval of illustrations, cover and print-ready files
  • Final price and commercial release
  • Public statements about authorship and the use of AI
