The claim

What Seretei is

Seretei is the manga, comics and graphic-narrative producer of the Scriba group. It develops original visual stories — memorable characters, consistent universes — through human teams and specialised AI subagents, and prepares its intellectual properties for publication, translation, commercialisation and adaptation to other media.

The system

Production pipeline

Seretei's work runs through seven stages, each executed by several of its 27 AI subagents.

  1. Concept & acquisitionReceive concepts, scripts and portfolios; discover writers, artists, colorists and letterers; assess originality, audience, format and standalone / series / universe scope; spot adaptation opportunities.
  2. Script & universeBuild the premise, genre, tone and central conflict; write the technical script and split it into arcs, chapters and episodes; design episode endings that pull the reader on; document the visual rules of the universe.
  3. Design & storyboardDesign characters with recognisable silhouettes, turnarounds, expressions and wardrobe; design cities, interiors, vehicles, objects and creatures; turn the script into panels, pages and double spreads.
  4. Art, colour & letteringSketches, backgrounds and characters; final lines, tones and colour; light and motion effects; balloons, captions and sound effects; readable typography and reading order (right-to-left or left-to-right); editable master files.
  5. Editing & quality controlCheck script and continuity; catch visual errors in characters, wardrobe, objects and settings; proofread; verify bleed, margins, resolution and colour profiles; approve digital and print proofs.
  6. Publish with ScribaPrepare interiors, covers and formats; print-on-demand and digital versions; volume editions, numbering and credits; ISBN and metadata through Scriba; archive originals and finals.
  7. Market & adaptProduct pages, categories and keywords; teasers, previews and launch campaigns; episodic distribution; collections and special editions; reader communities; sales, reading and retention analytics; transmedia adaptations.

An executive producer coordinates each project and assigns the 27 AI subagents, which prepare and recommend.

Transmedia

Adaptations

A selected property can become motion comics, anime microseries, film or TV projects, audio manga and dramatisations, video games and interactive experiences, and physical products — produced with Great Studios. Seretei supplies the visual bible, character designs, canon, scripts and reference storyboards.

All adaptations must preserve the canon, the visual identity and the intellectual property of the original work. International localization runs six controls:

  • Narrative translation
  • Sound-effect adaptation
  • Cultural review
  • Lettering review
  • Canon control
  • Final visual approval
