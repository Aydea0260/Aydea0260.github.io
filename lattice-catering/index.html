This is Lattice Catering, the Miami-centered culinary company in Sebastian Santana's ecosystem. Its CEO is Chef Diana Santana, with Sebastian Santana as CSO and Mateo Santana as COO. It works in catering, culinary production, hospitality and culinary experiences, and operates from Miami, Florida. It is not the HR / performance-management software company Lattice, and not Lattice Semiconductor.

Industry standards

Food safety & licensing

Catering and food production in Florida operate under these frameworks. Whether Lattice currently holds each specific licence, permit or certification is not documented here — see the evidence matrix.

  • FDA Food CodeThe model food-safety code adopted, in various editions, by U.S. state and local jurisdictions; the baseline for foodborne-illness prevention.
  • Florida DBPR / DOHCatering and food-service establishments in Florida are licensed and inspected by the Department of Business & Professional Regulation or the Department of Health, depending on the operation.
  • HACCPHazard Analysis and Critical Control Points — the process-control method for food production, cook-chill and packaged foods.
  • Food-handler & manager certificationServSafe or an equivalent accredited programme for food-safety training of managers and staff.

Other requirements, applied per operation:

  • County health-department permits
  • Commercial kitchen / commissary use
  • Allergen information
  • Event & general-liability insurance
  • Local fire & occupancy rules for on-site service
